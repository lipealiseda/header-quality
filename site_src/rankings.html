Chapter 5

Player rankings

Every player with at least 100 aerial duels and a known height, ranked by duels won above what the model expected. This is the one header metric that passed the reliability test in chapter 4.

How to read the table

Above expected is extra duels won per 100, after shrinking toward average. The model behind it already knows both players' heights, positions, the delivery and the location, so a high number means a player wins more than his size and situation explain. The likely range shows where his true number probably sits, with an 80% chance. When the whole range is on one side of the middle line, the player is clearly above or below average.

Most duels come from the 2015/16 season, so clubs and heights reflect that time.
